# FF3 V26.17: evaluate qualified opportunities across routes

The default and competitive loaders share this change. X reloads main, which pins `ff3-v26.17`. There are no new HUD controls, native hooks, lead edits, catch-height assumptions, remote formats or other-game changes. The previous implementation remains available at immutable tag `ff3-v26.16`.

## Findings and changes

MOD/TRUST previously relaxed only `burnCandidate` trajectories. That flag requires forward receiver motion. A stable crosser or comeback could meet the selected endpoint/lane/race/reach/route criteria and still receive SAFE-only treatment because of movement direction. MOD/TRUST now apply their existing numerical limits to those qualified catch windows too. SAFE's limits are unchanged. All Bullet variants, conditional native covered Jump and trusted Mag still require strict coverage. Current defender motion, direct lane, receiver reach, route stability, no prevent threat, AFK/team/filter/LOS/field eligibility and final native calculations remain necessary. These are application risk policies, not calibrated interception probabilities.

The shared layered Fit branch also repeated SAFE endpoint/lane thresholds after the selected policy had already graded the candidate. It now uses that successful policy grade. It still requires arrival before the modeled defender, two actual threatened Bullet/Dime alternatives, no known safe non-Fit alternative and fresh counterfactuals before the actual final Fit. Timing-rescue Fit retains its existing conditions. Unsupported or missing alternatives cannot be invented as unsafe. A safe ordinary completion can still veto Fit. No arbitrary Y offset, mode-force or bypass is added.

Native planning remains scheduled for two receivers, with at most five planning attempts and eight native calls per frame. Previously only that pair could win evaluation, even when another eligible receiver had a still-fresh native tuple. The planner now regrades other eligible receivers' existing fresh tuples against current defender data. Their model/possession/LOS generation and 0.36-second age checks still apply. The winner always receives a new native calculation and final live validation; an old cache never authorizes a packet. This adds bounded arithmetic over resident fresh tuples and no native solves, workers, per-frame Workspace scans or UI rows. Phone cost remains unmeasured.

Between releasable receivers, shorter completion distance now takes precedence over the `clearBurn` label. Existing ordinary-Bullet comfort preference, DEEP/SHORT filters and target-switch hysteresis remain. An unsafe short player cannot beat a releasable deeper player. A burn label still helps rank unreleasable manual-lock candidates.

Blocked history now retains selected Risk, Ping, direct lane, defender/receiver ETA, race advantage, lateral/turn data, over-top state, Fit reason/comparisons, plan reason and release-block status. CurrentSettings also records RiskMode and updates when it changes. Existing bounded storage/throttles and private credentials separation remain. The 75-ms observed-takeoff target and full-power flight comfort policies remain.

## Validation and remaining limits

37 current suites / 2,208 mocked assertions pass, including 96 new checks. Tests replay V26.16 versus current policy for non-forward routes; exercise an actual native-shaped MOD crosser release with observed-jump timing; preserve strict Bullet/covered Jump/Mag, ordinary-alternative Fit vetoes and hard rejection criteria; reproduce the overlooked third receiver and refuse expired tuples; check short-completion ranking, bounded solves, cleanup and richer retained diagnostics. Existing mode, possession, LOS/endzone, timing, screen, history, companion and loader checks pass. The bundle is rebuilt and checksum-verified.

These checks prove local decision/control contracts. They do not establish live safety, catch windows, player readiness, phone frame timing, pursuit-model calibration or runtime cost. The conservative pursuit disk still permits instantaneous defender turning, and inherited body/jump reach is uncalibrated. This release addresses demonstrated selection restrictions without loosening Bullet safety or inventing defender reaction delays. Runtime and bundle syntax are validated through the existing Lua 5.4 mock harness; no real Roblox throws, chat, currency use or teleport tests were run.
